## Core Value Props — 3 Blocks

**Section Headline:**
Built for instructors who teach without an army behind them.

**Block 1: Your Methodology, Not the Internet's**
Generic AI tools pull answers from everywhere. Maize only knows what you upload. When a student asks about discounted cash flow, they get your framework, your notation, your examples — not a random blog post.

**Block 2: Teaches Thinking, Not Answers**
Maize doesn't hand students solutions. It asks guiding questions, offers calibrated hints, and adapts based on how many times a student has tried. The goal is understanding, not copy-paste.

**Block 3: You Stay in Control**
Add materials, remove them, update them anytime. Every course gets its own isolated TA. You see what students are asking, where they're struggling, and what topics need more attention in your next lecture.

## FAQ Section

**Is Maize safe to use with my course materials?**
Your materials are encrypted, isolated per course, and never used to train AI models. Students in one course cannot access materials from another.

**Do I need my university's IT department to set this up?**
No. Maize is a standalone tool. You sign up, upload your materials, and share a link with your students. There's nothing to install and no LMS integration required.

**Will Maize give students the answers to my problem sets?**
Not unless you want it to. By default, Maize is designed to guide students toward understanding rather than hand them solutions. You control how much direct help it provides.

**What file types can I upload?**
PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint slides, and plain text files. If your course materials exist as files on your computer, Maize can use them.

**What if I teach multiple courses?**
Each course gets its own separate TA with its own materials. Students only see what's relevant to their course.

**Can my human TAs use it too?**
Yes. You can invite co-instructors and TAs to manage the course alongside you.
